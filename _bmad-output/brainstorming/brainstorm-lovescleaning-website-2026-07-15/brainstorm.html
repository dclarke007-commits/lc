🧪 Substitute · Combine · Adapt · Modify · Put-to-use · Eliminate · Reverse
SCAMPER

Seven transformation verbs applied to the funnel, the payment, the receipt, the FAQ — swapping, fusing and deleting parts until the workflow gets radically simpler.

SubstituteCombineAdapt Modify/MinifyPut to useEliminateReverse
SubstituteReplace the booking form with a conversational SMS/WhatsApp agent: "Want your usual biweekly Tuesday?"
SubstituteSwap star reviews for auto-generated before/after photo galleries per job — visual proof beats text.
CombineScheduling + route: booking a slot auto-sequences her day by address.
CombinePayment + review: the review link IS the receipt; no receipt without seeing the review prompt.
CombineClient notes + gate/lock codes into a geofenced job sheet that surfaces only on the day, at the address.
AdaptRestaurant no-show deposit: booking requires a card hold; auto-charge cancellation fee inside 24h.
AdaptAirline dynamic pricing: cheaper slots on slow days, premium prime Saturdays; fills the week evenly.
Put to useTurn the FAQ into an interactive "will you clean my place?" quiz that qualifies and books in one flow.
MinifyOnboarding to a single link: 3 questions, card on file, booked in under 60 seconds.
ModifyClient history becomes a portable "cleaning passport" the customer owns; preferences travel if they move.
EliminateNo invoicing — auto-charge card-on-file at job completion, receipt texted; never chase payment.
EliminateNo homepage — replace it with one smart booking link she texts/shares; the site is the funnel only.
ReverseTwo-way rating: the cleaner rates the customer too (messy? pets loose? tip?) so she can fire bad clients.

5LENS
⛓️ Forcing Functions
Extreme Constraints

Impossible limits breed elegant answers. $0 budget. No training. Wet gloves. Dead-zone basements. A ten-second cap on every action.

💸 $0 tech budget
MVP is a Google Sheet + Calendly + Stripe payment links glued together by automation.
🚫 No tutorials
Any feature that needs a tutorial is cut. Zero training required.
🧤 Hands wet/gloved
Voice-driven: "mark this job done and charge the card."
📵 Offline basements
Job sheets cached locally in dead zones, sync later.
⏱️ 10-second rule
Any client action — book, reschedule, pay, refer — must take under 10s or it's redesigned.
📱 One-screen dashboard
The entire owner view fits one glanceable phone screen, between jobs.
🌱 No marketing spend, ever
Every growth mechanism must be organic / referral by design.
🏖️ Survives a 2-week vacation
Auto-pause recurring, notify clients, resume without losing anyone.

⚙️ Systems Thinking
The Flywheel

Stop thinking in features — think in loops. Every completed job should spin the wheel a little faster. Here is the engine at the centre of it all.

📸Photo → portfolio
⭐Review request
🎁Referral offer
🔁Rebook prompt
1 completed job auto-fires 4 actions

The core loop. A single finished clean automatically triggers four growth events — the portfolio grows, reviews pile up, referrals fire, and the next booking is set. No manual follow-up, ever.

🔒 MoatData compounding: preferences + history make each clean better → more reviews/referrals → lower CAC. The dashboard is the moat.
📍 Geo loopReferrals weighted to nearby addresses (bigger discount to a neighbor) → tighter routes → more jobs per day.
🔎 SEO loopAuto-published reviews feed service-area SEO pages that feed the booking link — a closed loop.
📅 Fill loopA cancellation instantly offers the slot to a waitlisted client — near-zero idle capacity.
📊 SmoothingSlow-day dynamic discounts smooth demand into predictable weekly income.
💰 FloatPrepaid memberships fund supplies/equipment upfront; the float is working capital.
🛡️ Owner healthTwo-way rating lets her fire bad clients — owner-retention as a longevity strategy.

✦ The Synthesis ✦

Three Breakthrough Connections

102 sparks converged on three ideas that changed the shape of the whole business. This is the payoff.

01

Geo-weighted referrals unify two levers

Weighting referrals toward nearby addresses collapses marketing and route-efficiency into a single lever — every new neighbor makes the route tighter and the next referral cheaper.

02

The dashboard is the MOAT

The owner dashboard isn't a back-office tool — it's the competitive moat. Compounding client data makes every clean better than a competitor's first clean could ever be.

03

Productize the tool itself

The true 10× is franchise-in-a-box — licensing the system to other solo cleaners so the tool, not the cleaning, becomes the business.

· And the cross-links that tie it together ·

💳
Card-on-file + auto-charge + prepaid membership together eliminate the entire payment-chasing problem and create predictable MRR.
⚙️
The per-job flywheel — photo → portfolio, review, referral, rebook — turns every completed job into a growth event.
🛡️
Two-way rating + hard capacity cap + client-concentration flag together protect the owner from burnout and single-client fragility.
✦ Recommended near-term direction ✦

Build a flywheel-centric booking + dashboard core — with card-on-file auto-charge, route-aware self-scheduling, and geo-referral at its heart. Treat franchise & co-op as the long-horizon vision.
