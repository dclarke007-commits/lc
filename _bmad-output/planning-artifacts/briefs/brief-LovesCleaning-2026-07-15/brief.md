# Product Brief: LovesCleaning

## Executive Summary

LovesCleaning is a phone-first booking engine and instrument panel for a solo cleaning business. Today that business runs on a notebook and a phone, and it operates at a third of its own capacity — 5 jobs a week against a possible 14 — not for lack of demand, but because work leaks away unseen between the inquiry and the rebooking. LovesCleaning exists to catch that leak, make it visible, and close it.

The product does three things a notebook cannot: it lets clients book and rebook in seconds instead of playing phone tag, it shows the owner exactly how full the week is and which clients have quietly gone cold, and it keeps a running record of who has paid and who still owes — all without forcing any change on how clients pay (cash stays king). The prize is concrete: filling the existing book nearly triples revenue, from roughly $4,000 to $11,000 a month, with no new hires and no ad spend.

Why now: the owner is at the exact inflection point where the notebook has become the ceiling. Every additional job makes the leak worse and the blind spots costlier. A simple, single-operator tool built around the real mechanics of this business — not generic field-service software — is the difference between staying stuck at 5 and running a full, legible book that can later carry a team.

## The Problem

A solo cleaner can fill 14 jobs a week. Today he does 5. His book is 64% empty — but not because the phone doesn't ring. It rings. The work leaks out between "they asked" and "they booked again."

The whole business runs on a notebook and a phone, and that is exactly why the leak is invisible. Inquiries come in and go unconverted. A one-time job goes perfectly and never rebooks. A weekly regular quietly skips, then stops — and it takes weeks to notice the slot went cold. Every one of those is revenue that was already in reach, lost to nothing more than the absence of a system to catch it. At $200 a job, the gap between 5 and 14 is roughly **$1,800 a week — about $7,000 a month of capacity sitting idle**. Filling the existing book nearly triples revenue (from ~$4,000 to ~$11,000 a month) without hiring a single additional cleaner. It is the single largest number in the business — and it is entirely self-inflicted, the price of not being able to *see* it.

Payment leaks too, quietly. Cash-at-completion mostly works, but when a client comes up short, the money floats — averaging ~$500 unpaid or late every month. No record, no reminder, no follow-up. Just an awkward conversation the cleaner would rather not have.

The cost of the status quo is not a bad week. It is a business permanently stuck at a third of its own capacity, with no instrument panel to tell the owner which leak to plug first, or whether last month was better or worse than this one.

## The Solution

LovesCleaning is the instrument panel and booking engine that turns a notebook-and-phone operation into a business the owner can actually see and steer. It attacks the leak at all three points, and makes the leak visible for the first time.

**Catch the inquiry.** A real booking link replaces "text me." A client picks an open slot and it is booked — no phone tag, no forgotten follow-up, no inquiry falling through the cracks. Conversion stops depending on the cleaner remembering to call back.

**Close the loop on every job.** The moment a job is done, rebooking is one tap — "same time next week?" The one-time customer who would have vanished gets an effortless path back onto the calendar, and the weekly regular's next slot is already held. Rebooking becomes the default, not an afterthought — and the nudge arrives where the client already is: a WhatsApp or text, not one more app to download.

**See the whole business.** One screen answers the questions the notebook never could: How full is next week (jobs booked out of 14)? Which clients repeat, and which have quietly gone cold? Is this month ahead of or behind last? Who still owes money? This is the observability the owner asked for — not team-management dashboards, but a leak detector that shows exactly which slot to fill and which client to win back.

**Know what to take and what to pass.** Simple forecasting on top of the calendar tells the owner how much room is left this week and when a day is maxed — so new work is accepted with confidence instead of guessed at, and overbooking stops.

**Keep cash king, kill the float.** Payment stays cash-at-completion — no card processing, no behavior change forced on clients. The system simply records who paid and who came up short, flags anything outstanding, and nudges a reminder over WhatsApp or text so the ~$500 monthly float gets chased automatically instead of forgotten.

The through-line: catch what leaks, see what leaks, and stop guessing — all runnable from a phone, by one person, between jobs.

## What Makes This Different

The honest advantage here is not technology — there is no patent and no algorithm nobody else has. It is **fit, focus, and compounding data**.

- **Built by the operator, for the operator.** Every feature earns its place against one real book of business. There is no field-service-SaaS bloat, no modules for problems this owner doesn't have. The tool is exactly as big as the job.
- **Single-minded on the leak.** Most tools help you *run* jobs. This one is built to help you *not lose* them. The dashboard is a leak detector, not a wall of vanity charts.
- **Zero friction, zero behavior change.** Phone-first, operable by one person between jobs, reaching clients through WhatsApp or text they already use, and asking nothing new of them — cash stays king. Adoption cost is close to zero.
- **A moat that grows per job.** As the owner's client history accumulates — who repeats, who churns, seasonal rhythms — the forecasting and win-back sharpen. The data advantage compounds with every completed job, and it is owned by the operator, not rented from a platform.

The bet is execution speed and obsessive simplicity — nothing more, and nothing less.

## Who This Serves

**Primary — the solo cleaner-owner.** Damian, and operators like him: running everything alone, phone-first, currently on a notebook. He wants two things above all — a full book and clear numbers. Success for him looks like walking into a full week he didn't have to chase, and knowing his repeat rate, his utilization, and his outstanding cash without opening a drawer.

**The clients (booking side).** The people who hire the cleaner. They are not the buyer, but their friction *is* the leak: they want to book or rebook in seconds without a phone call. Removing their friction is how the owner's book fills.

**Secondary, later — additional cleaners.** As the business grows, other cleaners are onboarded onto the same instrument panel. This is a Vision-horizon audience, deliberately out of v1 (see Scope).

## Success Criteria

**North-star metric: repeat-booking rate** — the share of jobs that lead to a rebooking. If this rises, the leak is closing.

Supporting measures:
- **Utilization** — jobs per week climbing from 5 toward 14.
- **Revenue** — monthly trend from ~$4,000 toward ~$11,000, driven by fill, not price hikes.
- **Payment float** — the ~$500/month unpaid-or-late shrinking toward zero.

Leading indicators to instrument from day one:
- **Inquiry → booking** conversion rate.
- **One-time → repeat** conversion rate.
- **Regulars gone cold** caught within one week instead of several.

## Scope

**In (v1):**
- Instant booking link — clients self-book an open slot.
- Real-availability calendar with a hard 14-job weekly cap.
- One-tap rebooking + post-job "same time next week?" nudge.
- One-screen dashboard: utilization, repeat vs. lapsed clients, month-over-month revenue, who owes.
- Forecasting-lite: room-left-this-week and day-maxed signals.
- Cash ledger: record paid/owed per job, flag outstanding, send reminder.
- Messaging over channels clients already use (WhatsApp / text): booking confirmations, rebook nudges, payment reminders.

**Out (explicit):**
- Card processing / auto-charge (cash is king — deliberately dropped from earlier plans).
- A native mobile app — the product lives on phone-web plus WhatsApp/text clients already have; nothing to download.
- Multi-cleaner onboarding and team management (Vision, not v1).
- Advertising / lead generation (the problem is a demand *leak*, not a demand drought).
- Anything that does not touch the leak.

## Vision

Once the owner's own book runs full and legible, the same instrument panel becomes the backbone for growth. With capacity and demand finally visible, onboarding a second and third cleaner stops being a leap of faith — the numbers say when there is overflow work to hand off and where it is. Work routes by geography; forecasting spans a small team instead of one calendar.

The far horizon is a **franchise-in-a-box**: the playbook plus the software that takes any solo cleaner from notebook to full book, then from full book to a small team, on rails the founder has already walked. What begins as one person's leak detector becomes the operating system for a multi-cleaner cleaning business — grown deliberately, on data, one filled slot at a time.
